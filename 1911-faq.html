Sizing & Fit

Each product page lists a size chart based on neck, chest, and back-length measurements rather than breed or weight alone, since dogs of the same breed can vary a lot in shape. Measure your dog with a soft tape and compare against the chart for the specific product — sizing isn't identical across harnesses, coats, and collars.

If your dog measures between two sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for coats (to allow a base layer or extra coat underneath in cold weather) and sizing to the snugger fit for harnesses (for secure, correct pulling mechanics).

Most of our harnesses and belts have adjustable straps with a reasonable range built in, so a close match on the chart will usually still fit well once adjusted. If you're between sizes or have a breed with an unusual build, reach out to us before ordering and we'll help you pick the right size.

Materials & Care

It's a hydrostatic head rating — the outer fabric can withstand a 20,000mm column of water before moisture starts to press through. In practical terms, that's well beyond what a normal rainy walk will throw at it, keeping your dog dry in sustained wet weather.

Hand wash in cold water with a mild, non-detergent soap, then air dry away from direct heat. Avoid the washing machine and tumble dryer where possible — high heat and agitation can damage the water-resistant coating and insulation over time.

For insulated coats (Fellex® ECO-HSRP), let the item dry fully flat before storing, to keep the loft of the insulation intact.
