Est. 1911

In 1911, Roald Amundsen faced the harshest conditions on Earth to become the first human on the South Pole. He succeeded because he trusted his preparation and our gear, crafted with care and built to endure.

Over 100 years later, we continue that same spirit forward.

Every stitch, every detail has been refined with passion and precision, so you can trust your gear as much as Amundsen did. Whether you're venturing into the wilderness or approaching everyday life with the same passion for the unknown.

Let this be the beginning of your next everyday adventure with your loyal companion.
